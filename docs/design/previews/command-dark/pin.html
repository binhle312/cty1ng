P1 · One pin look for the whole appApprovedThe approved rail pin (board T cards 3 to 6) becomes the standard: the same glyph, the same four states, the same corner. Only the place changes. On the rail and on bot cards it is a badge on the top-right corner of the thing you pin (the tile, the card), never on a bot avatar, because the avatar's top-right slot shows the bot state. In the header bot card it is a 22 px button beside the gear, with the same glyph and colours (rest: amber glyph when pinned, muted when not). Tooltips everywhere: "Pin to top" and "Unpin".

P2 · Pins in the top headerApprovedToday the header has no pin. Proposal: the header uses the same order as the Bots page, pinned bots first, a short divider, then the others (by Recent activity or Created), like the rail. Header avatars get no pin mark: their top-right slot shows the bot state, and the divider already shows where the pinned group ends. To pin from the header: click the avatar, then the pin button in the bot card (here on Designer). Reorder of pinned bots is only on the Bots page and the rail, because a drag in the header adds the bot to the conversation (UI-2.4). Deferred reordering (UI-2.9) still holds: a pin while the pointer is in the header moves the avatar at the next click outside.
