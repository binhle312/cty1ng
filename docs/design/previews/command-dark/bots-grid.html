S3 · Pin, unpin and reorder pinned botsApprovedThe pin standard (step 2c) on cards. Hover an unpinned card: the faded pin badge shows on its top-right corner; hover the badge: amber with "Pin to top". A click pins the bot, and it goes to the end of the Pinned group. A pinned card shows the small pin mark; on hover it becomes the unpin badge. A click unpins it, and it goes back to its place in Others. Drag a pinned card to reorder: its slot becomes a dashed gap. A drag works only inside the Pinned group, Esc cancels. Each pin, unpin and drop saves at once, with no save bar, and the header follows at once (the deferred reordering of UI-2.9 still stops the header from moving while the pointer is in it). Keyboard: Alt+← and Alt+→ on a focused pinned card. Cursor (07:30): a pinned card shows the grab hand (it can move), and the closed hand while it moves; an unpinned card shows the normal arrow. A click opens the bot on both.
