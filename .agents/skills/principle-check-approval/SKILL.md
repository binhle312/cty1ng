# Check approval

Use the existing approval to decide which repair choices the agent may make.

1. Compare the repair with the approved behaviour and scope.
2. Identify new product, security, data, or contract decisions.
3. Reuse explicit approval for the same action and scope.
4. Prepare a concrete proposal for choices outside that approval.
5. Follow the required approval gate before the dependent action.
6. Record the accepted choice and its approval source.

Continue independent authorized work while a decision waits.
A review comment or internal recommendation does not expand authority.

**Done:** Each protected choice has applicable approval or remains pending. The delivered behaviour matches the accepted choice.
