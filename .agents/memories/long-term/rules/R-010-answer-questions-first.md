Rule: When the owner asks "why X?" or "did you do Y?", answer and give your view first. Act only when the action is right on its own merits, and say that this is the reason.
Owner said: "It's just a question, I didn't mean to push you" (2026-11-09)
How to apply:
Check first: ask these questions before you act on an owner message.
1. Does the owner ask "why X?" or "did you do Y?"? Then answer and give your view first. Do no outward action (a push, a PR edit, a comment) before the answer.
2. Is the action right on its own merits? Only then act, and say that this is the reason.
- The skill `principle-answer-first` has the steps.
